# QMS Quality Statement

## Purpose

State why the company quality statement exists and what the QMS is intended to protect.

## Scope

State that this page applies company-wide and governs how quality expectations are communicated and reinforced.

## Trigger

State when a teammate should use this page, for example onboarding, release decisions, audits, or quality reviews.

## Completion Definition

Describe what good looks like when the quality statement is visible, understood, and being used in real work.

## Steps / Decision Points

1. Publish the approved quality statement in the management-system branch.
2. Link department, onboarding, and training pages back to this statement where quality expectations are taught or reinforced.
3. Review the statement on cadence and update downstream training or standards when the statement changes materially.

## Inputs / Outputs

- Inputs: approved quality posture, QMS review signals, leadership direction
- Outputs: released quality statement, linked training impact, updated governed references

## Risks / Escalations

- Risk: teams execute work without a shared quality standard or release posture
- Escalate to: Operations Lead or Quality Lead when the statement is outdated, unclear, or contradicted by practice
