# <New Employee Onboarding Plan>

## Purpose

Describe what capability, readiness, and behavior this onboarding path should build for a new employee.

## Scope

State which department, role type, and onboarding window this plan covers.

## Trigger

State when a person enters the path, for example new hire start date, transfer into role, or recertification after extended absence.

## Completion Definition

State what readiness looks like before the employee may execute independent work.

## Steps / Decision Points

1. Complete the company foundation modules.
2. Complete the department ramp and system-specific orientation.
3. Complete role certification, observed practice, and required sign-off gates.

## Inputs / Outputs

- Inputs: offer accepted or role assignment, access approvals, released standards, onboarding schedule
- Outputs: completed onboarding record, role readiness decision, retained sign-off evidence

## Risks / Escalations

- Risk: employee executes work without sufficient context, tool discipline, or certified readiness
- Escalate to: department lead or onboarding owner when progress stalls, access is missing, or understanding is weak
