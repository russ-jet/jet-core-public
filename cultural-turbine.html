        Platform Guide

        How to use the platform

        Use this view as the day-to-day operating map for finding standards, checking authority, managing training acknowledgement, and preparing clean markdown uploads.

        Step 1

        Find the right page fast

        Start with the branch that matches the kind of truth you need, not the tool you happen to be using.

        • Use `Cultural Turbine` for mission, pillars, and operating logic.
        • Use `Departments` for role and process ownership.
        • Use `Shared Systems` when the question is tool-specific.
        • Use `Cross-Functional Flows` to understand handoffs.
        Step 2

        Use search and source authority together

        Search helps you find a likely page, but the `Source of Truth` section tells you which system actually owns the live record.

        • Search by standard, tool, ticket family, or department term.
        • Confirm whether Fusion, Odoo, Jira, Brain, Product Home, or Standard Work owns the record.
        • Follow linked authoritative references instead of duplicating instructions.
        Step 3

        Track training and sign-offs

        `Command Structure` ties standards to leads, crews, and acknowledgement status for revision releases, retraining, and knowledge-drift follow-up.

        • Use the command structure to see who owns acknowledgement follow-through.
        • Use the dashboard progress bars to see completion by team.
        • Use the sign-off cards to capture group or individual acknowledgement.
        • Leave retraining open until the current revision is acknowledged.
        Step 4

        Use the onboarding lane for new-hire readiness

        `Onboarding Lane` ties the QMS quality statement to company foundation, department ramp, role certification, and progress visibility.

        • Start with the QMS quality statement and company foundation.
        • Filter by department and job type to see the right ramp.
        • Use progress bars and milestones to see what remains before certification.
        Step 5

        Prepare uploads through the gate

        `Upload Gate` is the clean-entry checkpoint for new or revised markdown before it should be published into the governed library.

        • Start from the matching markdown template.
        • Keep the filename aligned to `document_id`.
        • Fill metadata, revision, training-impact, and supersede fields completely.
        • Resolve all blocking validation errors before upload.

        Recommended Use

        Who should do what in the platform

        Crew

        Find the current standard, follow the authoritative reference, and acknowledge revision-driven retraining when assigned.

        Leads

        Own training completion, confirm understanding, and escalate gaps, drift, or outdated standards into the improvement or change-control path.

        Owners

        Maintain page correctness, review cadence, revision control, and supersede handling for the pages they govern.

        Uploaders

        Use only approved templates, pass the gate cleanly, and submit uploads only within delegated authority.
